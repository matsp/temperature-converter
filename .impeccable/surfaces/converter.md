# Surface brief: converter (index.html)

- **Mode:** Operate. One screen; the visitor types a number and reads two more, in seconds.
- **Job:** anyone mid-task (recipe, weather, lab, spec sheet) converting between °C, °F and K. Success: the answer is readable as the last digit lands; no button, no popup.
- **Direction (chosen by Mats, round 2 "A: deep shade"):** weather map at night. The value's 10 °C isotherm band tints the whole page very dark; figures glow in the band colour; legend strip with a marker at the foot.
- **States:** typical value; cold and hot bands; long value (smaller figures); exactly absolute zero (no notice, marker off the scale); below absolute zero (off-map ground, ink figures, inline notice); empty (neutral ground, no marker); focus.
- **Boundaries:** spec scope only (three units, no history, no dropdown, no settings). Offline from file://, self-hosted fonts. Mockup: design/mockups/converter.html; tokens design/mockups/tokens.css; system DESIGN.md.
